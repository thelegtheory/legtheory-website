Policies

Shipping

Orders are normally dispatched within 1 working day after payment has been received.

Direct checkout is currently available for the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and France. For delivery to other EU countries, please contact us at hello@legtheory.com or via Instagram @thelegtheory before placing your order.

Once your order has been shipped, we will follow up by email with tracking details where applicable.

During temporary holiday dispatch periods or other exceptional circumstances, a different dispatch timeline may apply. Any such change will be clearly communicated on the website before purchase.

Returns & Right of Withdrawal

If you change your mind about an online purchase, you generally have 14 days from the day you receive your order to notify us that you wish to withdraw from the purchase.

After notifying us, you have a further 14 days to return the item.

You are welcome to carefully inspect and try the item for fit, as you would in a physical store. Please handle the product gently and avoid wearing, stretching, creasing, damaging or otherwise handling it beyond what is necessary to assess the product and fit.

If the product is worn, stretched, creased, stained, damaged, altered or otherwise handled beyond what is necessary to assess the product and this reduces its value, we may deduct the resulting diminished value from the refund.

For a full-order withdrawal, we will refund the purchase price together with the original standard delivery cost. If only part of an order is returned, the original delivery cost is not refunded.

Return shipping costs for a normal change-of-mind return are paid by the customer.

Please do not send any item back before receiving return instructions from us. The return address and instructions will be provided after your withdrawal request has been received.

Wrong or Faulty Items

If you receive an incorrect or faulty item, please contact us as soon as possible at hello@legtheory.com or via Instagram @thelegtheory .

This is handled separately from a normal change-of-mind return. We will work with you to resolve the issue and provide the appropriate return instructions.

Refunds

For returned goods, reimbursement may be withheld until we receive the goods back or you provide evidence that they have been sent back, whichever happens first. Returned items may be inspected to assess whether they have been handled beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and fit.

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method.
