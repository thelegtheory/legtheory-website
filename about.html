LEG THEORY

About

Leg Theory started from my pole dance hobby and a simple wish to create pieces I genuinely wanted to wear myself.

I have always loved styling, but I also know the feeling of having a wardrobe full of clothes and still wanting something new. I did not want Leg Theory to be about buying an entirely new outfit every time. I wanted to create pieces that could bring a different feeling to things you already own.

Each piece is designed to add something distinctive while still being clean, versatile and easy to style. Fabrics are chosen carefully, with quality and movement in mind. The idea is to give you more styling possibilities from your existing wardrobe, whether that means changing the silhouette, adding texture or simply making something familiar feel exciting again.

Leg Theory is an independent one-person label based in Amsterdam. I work in very small quantities and grow the brand carefully. Producing this way often means fewer factory options and higher costs per piece, but it allows me to stay close to what I am making and avoid producing more than I realistically need.

I also do as much as I can myself, from developing the website and organising orders to creative direction, photography and packing. Part of the reason is simple: I want to keep the pieces as accessible as I can while building the brand at a scale that makes sense.

Because of that, I really appreciate thoughtful purchases. If you are ever unsure about sizing, fit, styling or whether a piece will work for you, please message me before ordering. There is a real person on the other side, and I am always happy to help.

Leg Theory is still growing, experimenting and learning. I would rather build it carefully than pretend it is something much bigger than it is. Thank you for being here and for giving a small idea the space to become something more.

Macy ♡
