Finding your length

Measure in your heels, from the very top of your inner thigh down to where you want the mesh to finish, so there are no surprises. The long version (95 cm) gives a dramatic drape over the platform. The short version (80 cm) suits shorter legs, or anyone who likes their platform visible. Not sure? DM us and we'll help you choose.
