A long, flared silhouette in fine sheer mesh. Fitted through the thigh and opening into a full flare that falls over the platform, it moves with every step and spin.

Fine and featherlight, the mesh stretches with you and breathes through every routine, with a soft, second-skin feel against the leg.

Long ribbon ties finish the top of each leg. Knot them at the side for a secure hold, wrap them around the waist and cross them, or tie them to your shorts or bikini bottoms. Or leave them loose and let them float with your movement. There is no single way to wear them; the styling is yours.

Details

  • Material: 90% nylon, 10% spandex
  • Length: 95 cm
  • Upper-thigh fit: tested on 52–67 cm circumference. If you measure above 67 cm, message us before ordering.

Finding your length

Measure in your heels, from the very top of your inner thigh down to where you want the mesh to finish. The 95 cm length gives a dramatic drape and generous platform coverage. Message us if you're unsure.

Leg garters — optional

Within the 52–67 cm range, the ties alone hold the legwear in place. Add the garters for extra security or simply as a styling detail. Below 52 cm, we recommend the garters: wear them over the mesh and thread the ties through the loop for a secure hold.

Leg garters are available only with the mesh legwear.
